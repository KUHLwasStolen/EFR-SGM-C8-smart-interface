What do these settings do?

If you set any of the above values you will get a calculation on the main page of what you have paid for electricity or depending on how your net provider handles electricity that you supply to the grid how much you have earned or given away.

You can also only set some of these values and the according (unset) calculations will be omitted. The only required field is your currency.

If you have set a field before and then save the settings again while there is nothing in said field, this will not overwrite this setting. To fully reset enter "0" and save.

Press the icon besides the heading to get back to the main page. If the settings don't show even though it said "Success!" after saving, then your browser didn't fully reload the page and used a cached version. Reload/F5 to fix this.
